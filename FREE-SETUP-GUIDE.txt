FILES
- index.html: complete mobile-friendly storefront with original logo embedded.
- products/catalogue.json: catalogue starts empty.
- site-settings.json: tagline, home text, WhatsApp and social links.
- .pages.yml: Pages CMS editing configuration.

FREE PUBLISHING STEPS
1. Create a free GitHub account.
2. Create a PUBLIC repository named gorilla-labs-store.
3. Upload every file and folder from this ZIP into the repository root.
4. Create a free Cloudflare account. Choose Workers & Pages > Create > Pages > Connect to Git.
5. Select gorilla-labs-store and deploy. Cloudflare gives a free *.pages.dev address.
6. Open app.pagescms.org and sign in with GitHub.
7. Install Pages CMS for your repository. It reads .pages.yml.
8. In Pages CMS, open Products to add, edit, or delete products and upload photos.
9. Open Store settings to add WhatsApp number, Instagram URL and Facebook URL.
10. Saving changes writes to GitHub; Cloudflare automatically redeploys the site.

ORDER MODEL AND LIMITATIONS
This is a static catalogue/cart. The cart runs in the visitor's browser and builds a WhatsApp
order enquiry. There is no online card payment, server-side order database, inventory control,
or customer account system. Payment remains directly arranged with you via WhatsApp/socials.

A free pages.dev address is provided; your own custom domain is a separate paid registration.
Third-party free services may change terms or suspend accounts. Keep a copy of your website files.
